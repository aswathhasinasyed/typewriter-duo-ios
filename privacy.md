# Privacy Policy

<p class="updated">Last updated: 8 November 2026</p>

Platen Writer ("Platen") is a typewriter app. It is built so that nothing you write
leaves your device unless you send it yourself.

## What Platen collects

Nothing. Platen has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it
makes no network requests.

## What stays on your device

- **Your pages, folders and settings** are saved in the app's own storage on your
  device. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or your computer) like any
  other app data, under your Apple Account's own privacy terms.
- **Motion sensors** are read live to move the light on the keys as you tilt the
  device and, on a folding iPhone, to tell whether it is standing on a desk or held in
  your hands so the keyboard can fit. The readings are never stored or sent anywhere.
- **The hinge** on a folding iPhone is read only to choose the on-screen layout. Its
  state is never stored or sent anywhere.

## Sharing

When you choose Send, Share or Print, Platen hands your pages to the iOS share sheet or
print panel, and you choose where they go. Platen doesn't see or keep a copy of where
you send them.

## Children

Platen collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Move a document to the wastebasket to delete it (it is emptied after 30 days). Deleting
the app deletes everything Platen has saved on your device.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new
date.
