## Common questions

**How do I start a new line?**
On iPhone, tap the red **Return** key at the bottom right. On iPad and on a folding
iPhone, tap the chrome lever on the left of the carriage or drag it to the right; a
right swipe across the strip just above the keys does the same. On a hardware keyboard,
press Return.

**How do I fix a mistake?**
Backspace erases the character before the carriage. In **Assist** mode you also get
correction tape and undo. On paper, you can strike over a word with "x", or open the
page in Review and rub it out with the typewriter eraser.

**How do I type numbers and symbols?**
On iPhone, tap **#½** for the figures layer, and **ABC** to go back. In **Purist** mode,
1 types a lowercase L and 0 a capital O, as on a 1930s portable; **Assist** mode types
the modern figures.

**How do I get a capital letter?**
Hold Shift while you tap the letter, or tap Shift once for a single capital. Tap it
twice to lock it.

**The keys are dimmed.**
There's no paper in the machine. Pull the sheet down onto the platen, tap it, or
double-tap the right platen knob to feed one.

**I don't hear the keys.**
Check that **Sound** is on in the Lid (the gear icon) and that your volume is up. Platen
plays even when your iPhone is set to silent.

**How do I change the look?**
Open the Lid (the gear icon) to choose the typeface, the machine (five portables, each
with its own keys and desk) and the paper.

**How do I get my writing out?**
Open a page in Review (the page icon) and tap Send. Choose **PDF**, **Image** (a picture
of each page) or **Plain text**, either **as typed** or, for text, as a **clean copy**
with every correction applied.

**Where are my other documents?**
In the Cabinet (the folder icon). Swipe a card to frame it, take a carbon copy or move
it to the wastebasket.

**I'm on a folding iPhone. Why did the keyboard move?**
Platen follows how you hold the device. Propped up like a laptop, the paper sits on the
raised half and the keys on the flat half. Opened flat in landscape, the keys part at
the hinge. Stood like a book on a desk, the page is on one side and the machine on the
other; held in both hands, the keys curve around your thumbs. Closed, the outer display
shows the carrying case and settings.
